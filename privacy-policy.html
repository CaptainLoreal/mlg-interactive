Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2018

MLG understands that the protection of your privacy is of high importance to you. MLG is expressly committed to protecting your privacy and the personal data you may provide to MLG when you access or use materials on the website munichleadership.com, including the subscription pages of the site. Information that you provide to MLG in response to email requests for information or in any other form of contact, or that you share with MLG as part of the application process, is also treated in accordance with this privacy policy. MLG may amend this privacy policy from time to time at its own discretion. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Collection and Processing of Data

MLG collects data from users when they register for this website, request copies of publications, seek additional information regarding our services or employment opportunities, or register for conferences and other MLG-sponsored events. We use this data to tailor our website to the needs of its visitors. MLG will not sell such data to third parties, except that we may pass the data on to service providers acting on our behalf.

Email Addresses

We collect the email addresses of users who register to receive notifications by email or express interest in our services. We do not pass email addresses on to third parties and do not send unsolicited emails to the addresses we collect, except in the cases described in this privacy policy.

Cookies

MLG uses browser cookies that serve to track your visits to our website. Cookies are small text files sent from a web server to your browser and stored on your computer's hard drive. Most internet browsers allow you to manage or disable cookies.

This website uses Google Analytics. Google Analytics uses cookies that allow an analysis of the use of the website. The information generated by the cookies about your use of this website (including your IP address) is transmitted to and stored on a Google, Inc. server in the USA. You can prevent the use of cookies by adjusting your browser settings accordingly.

International Exchange of Information

MLG is a globally operating company. It may therefore happen that information collected via this website is exchanged between MLG offices or passed on to third parties around the world, including offices outside the European Economic Area. By using this website, you voluntarily consent to this information being shared internationally.

Security Measures

MLG uses technical and organizational security measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, manipulation or deletion. Only authorized employees and contractors have access to the data you have provided. However, MLG cannot guarantee that unauthorized persons will not gain access to your personal data.

Compliance with Laws

MLG complies with all applicable data protection laws and regulations. MLG may be compelled to disclose users' or customers' personal data to the judicial authorities without their express consent in order to comply with a court summons or a comparable judicial or administrative order.

Changing and/or Deleting Data

You may access and, where appropriate, correct, update and/or delete any personal information you provide to MLG through this website or by other means. To do so, please contact us using the contact details below.
