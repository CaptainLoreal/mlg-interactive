Our Services / Cultural Transformation

AI and robotics are pushing us into the biggest and fastest change we have seen in business history. Are your leaders prepared?

On one hand, being more open to the new, taking on a positive vision of the future, discovering opportunities rather than hesitating and "sit and wait": driving change is fundamentally a matter of one's individual mindset.

On the other hand, this often also involves the ability to speak uncomfortable truths, have difficult conversations, and implement unpopular decisions.

The magic lies in our deep expertise of Neuroscience and the application of creative tools to inspire curiosity.

We have helped entire corporations to get their leaders on their toes, as well as small Mittelstand companies and even departments. Every transformation is a well-curated set of cultural goals, strong commitment from the top, personal habit changes, role model behavior, and what we call "Guerilla activities" 🙂.

Typical situations where we love to help (examples):

  • You see the need for radical new behaviors in your area of responsibility: e.g., more agile, more responsible, bolder
  • You already have ideas how to restructure, and you want your leaders and workforce to support you
  • You are facing organizational restructuring and major changes that present your leaders with difficult emotional and communication challenges
  • You know you will move into a new strategic direction, and you want to get your workforce behind it lightning-fast

Speak to us about specific examples and let yourself be surprised.
