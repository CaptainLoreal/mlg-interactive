MLG Learning Platform

Your leadership journey — in one place.

Great development doesn’t end when the workshop does. The MLG Learning Platform is the digital companion to our leadership programs: it prepares participants before each module, deepens the work between sessions, and keeps every insight, tool, and material within reach long after the program ends.

A companion to every training we deliver

Every MLG program can come with its own digital home. Participants follow a clearly structured journey: modules, deep dives, pre-work, and exercises that mirror the live experience and extend it. Content unlocks automatically as the program progresses, so the right material is always there at the right moment.

All materials in one place

Slide decks, workbooks, documents, videos, and curated links, accessible any time, on any device.

Structured journeys

Programs organized into modules and deep dives, published on your schedule.

Progress at a glance

Participants see where they stand, and nothing gets lost between sessions.

Everything around the training, handled

The platform doesn’t just carry content: it carries the whole program.

Logistics

Venues, session dates, directions, and downloadable travel documents for every in-person module.

Facilitator profiles

Participants always know who they’re working with, and how to reach them.

1:1 coaching

Participants can connect directly with their facilitators for individual sparring sessions.

Personal notes

A private space to capture reflections alongside every lesson.

Built for the people who run programs

Behind the scenes, the platform makes program management simple: for us and for our clients.

Clients & cohorts

Each client gets their own branded space, and participants are organized into cohorts with access to exactly the right programs.

Roles that fit reality

Administrators, client HR admins, facilitators, and participants each see precisely what they need, nothing more.

Effortless onboarding

Invite participants individually or import whole cohorts at once, with enrollment happening automatically.

Smart communication

Automated, personalized emails keep participants informed about new content and upcoming sessions, with a one-click opt-out in every message.

Measure what matters

Anonymous evaluations are built in. Surveys go out automatically at the moments you define, results arrive in clear traffic-light dashboards, and cohorts can be compared side by side: so you see not just that development happened, but where.

Securely hosted in Germany

Your data stays where it belongs. The platform is hosted exclusively in Germany, fully GDPR-compliant, with security built in from the ground up: from encrypted access to strict role-based permissions. No third-country transfers, no compromises.
