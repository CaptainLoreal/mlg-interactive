Our Target Groups

Who benefits from our know-how?

Large corporations, Mittelstand, start-ups and scale-ups — we work with companies of all sizes.

Large Corporations

Because we know the world of corporate executives well:

pressure from shareholders, quarterly reports with ever-increasing expectations; dealing with personal uncertainty; tight deadlines and the need for sound decisions; concerns about sustainable team performance; top-down versus bottom-up approaches; the opportunities and challenges of matrix structures; rivalries involving both overt and covert power struggles; the sometimes difficult question of responsibility and ownership; the ambivalent attitude toward the growing importance of artificial intelligence; and the important and often underestimated role of HR.

Mittelstand Companies

Because we value the long-term, cross-generational perspective of family-owned businesses:

the unique corporate culture with its strengths, which are often underutilized; the sense of connection and loyalty to the workforce and to customers, with all its advantages and disadvantages; the often clear positioning and the associated concern that not everyone is pulling in the same direction; the directness and the often intuitive approach; the ambivalent relationship with AI; the rapid maneuvers that perhaps too often manifest themselves in stressful “firefighting” measures.

Start-ups and Scale-ups

Because we love the founders’ dream:

their entrepreneurial ambitions; their unbridled drive to move forward; their enthusiasm for experimentation, coupled with the fear of not moving fast enough; their passion for the product; the uncertainty about having the right people on board and filling roles optimally; the extremely rapid growth and the inevitable growing pains; the uncertainty in assigning responsibilities; building teams, structuring tasks, and the question of balancing trust versus control; the pervasive openness to digital tools; communication that is too often unstructured; and the question they often ask themselves: what does professional leadership actually look like for us?

We bring together the best of these three worlds, and each one benefits from the success models of the others.
