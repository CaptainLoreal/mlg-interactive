Personal profile

Jan is a licensed psychologist and executive coach with practice across law, healthcare, architecture, advertising, energy, government, military, education and start-up environments. She is a Center for Creative Leadership executive coach and an ICF Certified Coach.

She holds a Ph.D. in Community / Clinical Psychology from the University of Nebraska with a minor in Program Evaluation, and a B.A. in Psychology (Highest Distinction) from the same university. Her clinical credentials include an APA-Approved Internship and Research Fellowship at the Seattle Veterans Administration.

Jan focuses on senior leadership team alignment and strengthening, coaching company founders and executives in startups and established companies, strategy coaching for grant pitch competitions, and leadership development across every level from first-time managers to C-Suite. She serves on the Beanstalk Foundation board and the Women’s Council of the Leeds School of Business at the University of Colorado, Boulder.
