Team / Martina Bohnenstiel

Personal profile

Martina holds a Diploma in Business Administration from the European Business School (ebs) in Oestrich-Winkel, with study periods in London and Paris. She is a certified Systemic Coach (DBVC) and has built a diverse career spanning new media, consulting, telecommunications, and distance selling.

Martina Bohnenstiel is an Executive Leadership Consultant, Systemic Coach and Facilitator with more than 15 years of experience helping leaders and organizations navigate complexity, transformation and uncertainty.

Her strength is not simply developing leaders. It is enabling leaders and teams to remain clear, connected and effective when pressure, change and conflicting interests threaten performance.

Having worked as both a business leader and consultant, Martina combines strategic thinking with real-world leadership experience. She has designed and facilitated leadership programs, executive workshops and organizational transformations across industries, always focusing on one question:

How do people perform at their best when the environment is at its most demanding?

Beyond leadership development: Martina believes that sustainable performance is not created by adding more leadership tools. It is created by helping people access their existing capabilities when it matters most.

She facilitates conversations that create clarity instead of confusion, alignment instead of politics and commitment instead of compliance.

Whether working with executive teams, leadership groups or organizations undergoing transformation, her goal is always the same:

Creating the conditions in which people can think clearly, decide confidently and lead effectively.
